How to read Daymark

Transparency is more useful than a perfect-looking graph. These charts visualize the stored measurements; they do not manufacture data for missing dates.

Source, not guesswork

Each metric identifies its origin. Direct measurements, device-recorded readings and device estimates are explicitly distinguished.

Derived views are labeled

Daily workout totals and observed rolling averages are calculated on screen for readability. The underlying dataset retains individual observations.

Gaps and freshness stay visible

Missing days aren't zeros. Partially recorded days are labeled. The last imported date helps show when a source may be stale.

What I'm building toward

The idea is to link behaviors with outcomes while keeping observation, estimation and interpretation separate. These are planned areas, not claims about the current data.

Food, as a measurable input

Meal templates and recipe versions, estimated calories and nutrients, ingredient quality and source provenance—so dietary changes can be compared over time without claiming exact intake from guesses.

Training, as a long-term experiment

Workouts organized by modality, duration and intensity, plus recovery and performance markers. Sessions first, then clearly defined aggregates and progress comparisons.
